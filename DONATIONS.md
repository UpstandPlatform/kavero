## Before you send

- **Check the network.** Send only on the network listed for an address. The ETH address works on Ethereum, Base, BNB Smart Chain and Arbitrum; tokens sent on any other network may be lost.
- **Copy, don't type.** Copy the address from this file in the [official repository](https://github.com/UpstandPlatform/kavero/blob/master/DONATIONS.md) and compare the first and last few characters before confirming.
- **Donations are gifts.** They don't buy support, features or priority, and they can't be refunded. Nobody from the project will ever message you asking for a donation or for "verification" payments.
- These are the only donation addresses. If you see different ones elsewhere, they aren't ours. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/UpstandPlatform/kavero/issues) to let us know.

## Other ways to help

Money isn't the only way to help:

- **Star** the [repository](https://github.com/UpstandPlatform/kavero) so others find it.
- **Report bugs** and suggest features with the [issue templates](https://github.com/UpstandPlatform/kavero/issues/new/choose).
- **Contribute** code, templates, translations or docs: see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Share** catalogs you made with Kavero and tell us what you'd like it to do next.

Thank you!
